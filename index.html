#WritersCoffeeClub Oct. 16: How do you feel about people reading your works without buying a personal copy?

I am completely fine with it.

1. While the income from book sales is nice, I basically consider them marketing vehicles for my Patreon campaign where the bulk of my income comes from.

2. The more people in the English-speaking world become aware of and interested in old German folk tales, the better for me. I mean, where else are they going to get a fix for new translations of these tales?

3. I've posted the bulk of my translations online, for free, under a Creative Commons Zero license anyway (although they are missing the additional commentary and explanations that my books and Patreon posts provide). I _want_ as many people as possible to read my tales (see point 2)!
